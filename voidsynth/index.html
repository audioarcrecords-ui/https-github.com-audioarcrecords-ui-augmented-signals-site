What is VoidSynth?

VoidSynth is an AI-powered wavetable synthesizer VST3 plugin for Windows. Unlike conventional synths that give you fixed oscillators and preset-based workflows, VoidSynth fuses deep sound design architecture with a built-in AI engine that generates, morphs, and suggests patches in real time — without ever leaving the plugin.

Two independent oscillators — each switchable between wavetable, sample, and granular modes — feed into a full modulation architecture: triple LFOs, a resonant filter with drive and vowel formant shaping, and the MUTANT waveshaper for FM, waveshaping, and chaos modulation. Then eleven real-time FX modules take your sound further: granular freeze, V.GRAIN timestretch, spectral glitch, XY pad modulation, arpeggiator, multi-band spatial control, and AURA spatial field processing.

How the AI Synthesis Works

VoidSynth's built-in AI engine is not a gimmick. It's a real-time system trained on synthesis parameters that understands how sound design works. Give it a descriptor — "dark, evolving pad" or "aggressive bass" — and it builds a patch that matches. Hit Morph and it generates new variations of your current patch while preserving its character. Let it Evolve and the patch changes over time, creating sounds that were impossible to design manually.

This is AI synthesis as a creative partner, not a replacement for skill. Every AI-generated patch is fully editable. The goal is to get you to interesting sounds faster — then let your ear and hands take it the rest of the way.

How does the AI patch generation work?

VoidSynth's AI engine generates synthesis parameters in real time based on your input. You can describe a sound, morph your current patch into something new, or let it evolve autonomously. All generated patches are fully editable.

What's the difference between wavetable, sample, and granular mode?

Wavetable mode scans through waveforms for harmonic evolution. Sample mode plays back loaded audio with pitch tracking. Granular mode breaks audio into tiny grains for timestretch, pitch, and textural effects. VoidSynth lets you switch each oscillator between all three independently.
